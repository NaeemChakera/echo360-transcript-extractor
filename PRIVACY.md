# Privacy Policy — Echo360 Transcript Extractor

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

Echo360 Transcript Extractor collects no personal data.

## What the extension does
- When you click **Download**, it reads the list of recordings and their transcripts from Echo360, using the Echo360 session already open in your browser.
- It builds a ZIP file on your computer and saves it with Chrome's downloads feature.

## What it stores
- Your format preferences, in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`).
- The progress of the current download, in session storage, which is cleared when the browser closes.

None of this leaves your browser except through Chrome's own sync of your settings, if you have Chrome sync turned on.

## What it does not do
- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing history or course content.
- It does not use analytics or tracking, and it does not contact any server other than Echo360.
- It does not read or store passwords.
